Popular Sri Lankan Foods

Explore some of the most loved dishes from Sri Lanka.

Kottu Roti

Kottu is made by chopping flatbread called roti together with vegetables, spices and other ingredients. It is a very popular street food in Sri Lanka.

Hoppers

Hoppers are thin, crispy pancakes made from fermented rice batter and coconut milk. They can be eaten with curry or sambol.

Rice & Curry

Rice and curry is one of the most common traditional meals in Sri Lanka. It can include several vegetable, meat or fish curries.
